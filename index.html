English Version

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MAGNUM LIFE GENERATOR (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles user and device data. We are committed to transparency and user privacy.

2. Data We Collect

We do not require user account registration in the App.

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads (banner/interstitial/rewarded). Through AdMob and related services, certain data may be collected automatically, such as:

  • IP address (may be used to estimate approximate location)
  • User product interactions (e.g., app launches, taps, video views)
  • Diagnostic information (e.g., app performance metrics)
  • Device and account identifiers (may include Advertising ID / App Set ID)

Reference: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. How Data Is Used

  • Advertising and ad delivery
  • Analytics and app improvement
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance

4. Data Sharing

Data may be processed/shared with Google AdMob and its service providers according to Google’s policies. We do not sell personal and sensitive user data.

5. Security

Data handled by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS), per Google documentation.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts in this App. If you have a privacy request, contact us at mmluckyteam@gmail.com. Third-party data retention is governed by the third party’s own policies.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children. If your store listing target audience includes children, additional Play Families rules apply.

8. Third-Party Links

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Help Center

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

  • 診斷資料（例如：效能與穩定性指標）
